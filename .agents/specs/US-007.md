# US-007: Choose Storage for WoW and Its Data

> The heavy/light validation amendment to AC-05/FR-005 was approved on
> 2026-10-25. Its implementation and physical acceptance remain pending; other
> storage paths retain their existing validation policy.

## As a

user of the Android launcher

## I want

to choose where the client and its `Data` directory are stored, including a
compatible USB volume

## So that

I can use removable storage without automatically duplicating the client.

This records the explicit request to implement storage selection and the approved
correction limiting destinations to Android-permitted locations, without moving
existing data. See the [storage plan](../planning/done/launcher-client-storage-plan.md).

## Use Cases

- **US-007-UC-01**: View destinations, capacity, path, and client/data state.
- **US-007-UC-02**: Select a destination and use it to prepare/launch.
- **US-007-UC-03**: Handle disconnected USB, empty destination, or invalid target.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **US-007-AC-01** (UC-01): Settings shows a visible Storage entry, current
  location, and app-specific volumes exposed by Android.
- **US-007-AC-02** (UC-02): selection is persisted by identity; the client, Data,
  detection, derived WTF, and Wine `A:` mapping use the same root.
- **US-007-AC-03** (UC-03): if the selected media is absent, there is no fallback
  or internal copy; an empty destination remains selected and shows that the client
  is not installed.
- **US-007-AC-04** (UC-02): changing destination does not delete, move, or duplicate
  the client, Data, or originals; it does not modify runtime/prefix/drivers or the
  credential store.
- **US-007-AC-05** (UC-02, UC-03): paths, mount, and critical files are revalidated;
  destination cannot change during preparation, launch, game session, or unconfirmed
  teardown.
  For explicitly admitted directed destinations during a sourceless CASC install,
  full checks run at admission/resume, before staging access and new segment opens,
  and before activation. Fresh selection/revision and root/base no-follow identity
  checks run before each durable group, including partial groups. Full ancestor,
  marker and volume/namespace checks may be deferred across groups until the next
  full boundary. Intermediate staging writes and delayed detection are accepted;
  a detected failure blocks group acknowledgement and raw retirement. This policy
  does not guarantee binding of an existing writer descriptor to a separately
  reopened force pathname under every concurrent substitution.
- **US-007-AC-06** (UC-01): Account remains visible and accessible while prepared
  but inactive, preserving the protected dialog and disabled sender.

## Functional Requirements

### US-007-FR-001

- **Derives from:** US-007-AC-01
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN Storage is opened in Settings, THE SYSTEM SHALL show permitted native
destinations with path, capacity, and state, without presenting SAF as an
executable path.

### US-007-FR-002

- **Derives from:** US-007-AC-02
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN a valid destination is confirmed, THE SYSTEM SHALL persist its identity and
invalidate prior preparation so that the next launch uses that root for `A:`.

### US-007-FR-003

- **Derives from:** US-007-AC-03
- **Pattern:** Conditional

WHEN the selected destination is empty or absent, THE SYSTEM SHALL reflect that
state and reject preparation without silently selecting another location.

### US-007-FR-004

- **Derives from:** US-007-AC-04
- **Pattern:** Ubiquitous

THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve existing data without automatic copying, migration,
deletion, or download as a side effect of changing the selector.

### US-007-FR-005

- **Derives from:** US-007-AC-05
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE I/O or an unfinished game session exists, THE SYSTEM SHALL prevent
destination changes and revalidate the identity of the volume/root/critical files
at the applicable boundaries below.

For a sourceless CASC install with an explicitly admitted directed destination,
THE SYSTEM SHALL perform full validation at admission/resume, before initial
staging access and each new segment open, and before activation. Before forcing
and checkpointing each durable group, including a partial group, it SHALL check
fresh selection/revision and root/base no-follow identities. Missing, linked,
wrong-type, changed or unprovable identities SHALL stop the operation without
acknowledging the group or retiring its retained raw sources. Staging writes
before that check are permitted; group count is not a byte/time detection bound.
Full ancestor, marker and fresh volume/namespace checks are deferred until the
next full boundary, potentially across multiple groups in one segment. Other
destinations, sourced updates and non-CASC paths retain their existing full checks.

### US-007-FR-006

- **Derives from:** US-007-AC-06
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE the launcher is prepared and no game or I/O is active, THE SYSTEM SHALL
allow access to Account without unprotecting fields or enabling autologin.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **US-007-NFR-001**: metadata snapshots are bounded; do not traverse all of Data
  or calculate client hashes in the UI. PE/build validation runs off the UI thread.
- **US-007-NFR-002**: no root, all-files permission, arbitrary paths in intents, or
  fake URI-to-path conversion. Subtree-wide link/special-file checks apply at full
  validation boundaries; lightweight identity checks and individual owned-file
  opens retain their no-follow/type guards. Deferred full checks do not imply
  equivalent detection coverage between boundaries.
- **US-007-NFR-003**: process ownership and leases use tokens; selection/cancellation
  changes are linearizable; preserve other container drives.

## Assumptions

- The client and Data share one root. Separating them with symlinks is out of scope.
- “Internal” means the app-specific location already used by the proof of concept;
  it does not duplicate another root.
- Selecting a destination does not imply that the client is downloaded or complete.

## Open Questions

- App-specific USB was used in C112 staging (see the
  [downloader handoff](../../docs/downloader-handoff.md)); running Wine from USB,
  hot-unplug, and retaining the selection after reboot remain pending. Staging
  alone does not close them.
- Full installer, incremental updater, and runtime on a new phone remain separate
  gates and are not promised by adding the selector.

# US-004: Use the Functional Launcher

## As a

user of the functional Android ARM64 proof of concept

## I want

a clear welcome screen and to play with a physical gamepad, fullscreen display,
and accessible keyboard

## So that

I do not need diagnostic controls or a touchscreen gamepad during normal use.

This story records only UX1–UX4 and UX6 from the
[approved plan](../planning/arm64-launcher-app-polish-plan.md), authorized on
2026-10-15. It does not define automatic login, client downloading, or updating.
The functional variant and its derived configuration do not replace the
experimental automated contract in US-003 or relax US-002 validation.

The user expanded the visual presentation: a WoW-inspired aesthetic, title
typography and ornamentation, and permission to use original generated imagery.
The implementation uses system serif typography, a dark background, gold/bronze/
burgundy colors, and original vector artwork (emblem, stars, and mountains); it
does not include official fonts or art. Future generated images must have
permitted provenance/use and must not degrade legibility, interaction, or
performance.

## Use Cases

- **US-004-UC-01**: View the welcome screen and explicitly start the functional
  proof of concept.
- **US-004-UC-02**: Use the game with a physical gamepad, adapt resolution to the
  phone, start with low graphics, retain graphics saved in-game, and explicitly
  restore the graphics defaults when wanted.
- **US-004-UC-03**: Open the keyboard, use additional game-control keys, and
  restore the game's presentation.
- **US-004-UC-04**: Close a game session and safely recover an unfinished guest
  reservation on a subsequent explicit Play request.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **US-004-AC-01** (UC-01): the app opens to a welcome screen without starting
  Wine; it presents actual state, the primary action, progress/errors, and
  diagnostic settings separately.
- **US-004-AC-02** (UC-01, UC-02): the launcher is portrait, WoW is landscape,
  and exiting returns to portrait. During gameplay, Android bars and desktop
  frames are hidden where applicable without distorting the image or misaligning
  input. The approved display-fit behavior measures the usable landscape area
  and coordinates the Wine desktop and WoW resolution. Settings defaults to
  Automatic and offers lower internal resolutions/Native, clearly labeled as
  resolution rather than graphics quality. The selector adapts the guest to the
  phone's usable-area aspect ratio to remove avoidable black bars without stretching,
  cropping game content, or misaligning input; the full option row is selectable.
- **US-004-AC-03** (UC-02): initial graphics use the lowest visible quality level
  (1), disabled shadows, and a 30 FPS limit, with gamepad mode enabled. Graphics
  changes saved inside the game, including quality, shadows, and FPS limits, are
  retained between sessions instead of being reset by preparation, app reopening,
  APK updates, or resolution changes. The original configuration and unrelated
  fields in the derived profile are preserved.
- **US-004-AC-04** (UC-02): no touchscreen gamepad overlay appears, and the
  physical gamepad input path is preserved.
- **US-004-AC-05** (UC-03): a small button with a keyboard icon and accessible
  label opens the real IME, allows typing in WoW, and does not capture the rest
  of the screen.
- **US-004-AC-06** (UC-03): closing the IME or returning to the app restores
  fullscreen without stealing focus from the keyboard while it is open.
- **US-004-AC-07** (UC-01): Play integrates local preparation and reuses the
  installed environment when the app is reopened, with no separate manual
  preparation action. It does not check for updates or start Wine merely because
  the app was opened. User-facing text stays brief, with details available on
  demand. Resolution and graphics-reset actions automatically discard an idle,
  unlaunched preparation and its consent rather than requiring the user to manage
  internal preparation state. Actual preparation or conflicting IO remains excluded
  until it has finished; no late result may bypass that exclusion.
- **US-004-AC-08** (UC-03): while gameplay is active and the real Android IME is
  visible, an app-owned, horizontally scrollable accessory row appears above it
  within the usable display area. It provides accessible controls for Escape,
  Tab, Ctrl, Alt, the four direction keys, and hiding the IME. Each control has
  a touch target at least 48 dp wide and high. Escape, Tab, and direction taps
  send a press and release without dismissing the IME; Ctrl and Alt remain
  pressed until toggled off and are released when the IME closes or gameplay
  exits. The hide control is visibly labeled “Ocultar” and has the accessible
  label “Ocultar teclado”.
- **US-004-AC-09** (UC-01, UC-04): on Play, the app attempts to recover an old
  guest reservation only for the selected, matching storage, using existing
  exclusion and ownership checks and fresh proof that its runtime is quiescent.
  Only completed recovery permits fresh preparation, validation, consent, and
  guest admission; no old ticket is reused as launch authorization.
- **US-004-AC-10** (UC-04): game shutdown attempts to close its owned reservation
  only after proven quiescence. Active or unknown runtime state retains exclusion
  and blocks a new launch, with distinct brief messages and a retry path. Recovery
  runs off the UI thread and is not duplicated by concurrent Play requests; it
  cannot recover an updater reservation, force processes, reset storage, or bypass
  a changed destination or ticket ownership.
  A process proven to remain an owned zombie (Z) with the same PID/starttime/UID
  across fresh identity checks, with Threads:1 in both group-status reads, is
  terminated, not a live runtime; a Z leader with other threads remains unproven. Missing exe of
  that corpse does not block closure. Identity/state races remain unknown.
  Shutdown requests normal termination of the session's own audio server through
  its control channel off the UI thread, bounded in time; audio is not ignored
  by name and a remaining live/unknown process still blocks recovery.
- **US-004-AC-11** (UC-02): an APK action labeled “Restablecer gráficos”, usable
  only with the game closed and existing storage/exclusion guards satisfied,
  restores the initial low/level-1, shadows-off, 30-FPS graphics preset for the
  next launch. It does not reset the selected resolution, account, controls,
  server, storage, or client data. If an unfinished guest reservation remains after
  the game closed, the action attempts the same matching-storage, ownership-checked,
  freshly quiescent recovery used by Play before restoring graphics. It requires no
  preliminary Play request, cannot recover an updater reservation, and cannot force
  a running game or treat unknown runtime state as closed.

- **US-004-AC-12** (UC-01): the current attempt exposes a brief truthful memory-patch
  state: disabled, pending search, searching, application verified, failed, or monitoring ended.
  A scan finding alone is not application confirmation. A terminal state distinguishes
  whether application was previously verified; ending monitoring does not imply the
  applied key was restored. Capture is diagnostic only. A failed game launch cancels
  its owned patch session, including Wine setup failure without a normal exit callback;
  closure failures stay visible and do not bypass native exclusion. Late results from
  an older attempt cannot overwrite the current attempt's state. Existing opt-in,
  write authority, safety checks and timing limits remain unchanged. In this functional
  variant, observed WoW startup is its identified main window mapped with content
  activity. This signal automatically starts patch searching without waiting for
  Enter; it proves neither native process identity, presented pixels, login readiness
  nor authentication. Window observations belong to the current attempt only.
  A bounded last-attempt summary records public patch counters and relative timings,
  including the existing assisted submission when performed, without account data,
  raw logs, additional user gates or authority over patching. Submission is not
  authentication and no manual Enter is inferred to be Login.

## Edge Cases

- IME remains open while focus changes or the app pauses/resumes.
- A 16:9 guest resolution is displayed on a wider panel; do not stretch to hide
  margins.
- Duplicate start tap, preparation failure, or Wine failure.
- Configuration contains duplicate keys or case differences.
- Physical gamepad disconnects/reconnects; do not enable the overlay by default.

## Functional Requirements

### US-004-FR-001

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-01
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the app opens, THE SYSTEM SHALL display the welcome screen without starting
the game until an explicit action, distinguishing verified state from pending
capabilities.

### US-004-FR-002

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-02
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE the game is presented, THE SYSTEM SHALL use fullscreen without distortion
or loss of correspondence between image and input coordinates, in landscape;
outside the game session, the launcher is presented in portrait.

The orientation transcribes the user's explicit request: “the launcher should be
portrait until WoW opens (in landscape).” The approved display-fit plan measures
after rotation and before creating the guest session, applying insets/cutout once.
The Wine desktop and derived CVar use the same per-launch frozen plan. Automatic
targets height 720, lower resolution 540, higher resolution 900, and Native the usable area;
none exceeds the usable dimensions or changes the active session when Settings
changes. Labels describe resolution, not an in-game quality preset. Resolution
changes do not reinitialize the user's saved graphics. Avoidable internal margins
must be diagnosed at the presentation boundary rather than hidden with stretch or crop.

### US-004-FR-003

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-03
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN a launcher-owned derived configuration is initialized for the first time,
THE SYSTEM SHALL initialize the lowest-quality/shadows-off/30-FPS graphics preset
idempotently without modifying the original, `Config.wtf`, or fields outside the
preset. An existing derived profile is not a first initialization merely because
the APK was updated or the next session is being prepared.

The technical value for visible quality level 1 is `graphicsQuality=0`; gamepad
is enabled with the gamepad UI style, foreground/background limits are 30 FPS,
D3D11 is selected, and effects are minimized per the technical reference. Gamepad
and runtime-owned controls remain separate from user-editable graphics. This
does not promise sustained 30 FPS or complete ability bindings. Resolution is
preserved or adapted without stretching.

### US-004-FR-004

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-04
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE this functional variant is in use, THE SYSTEM SHALL hide the touchscreen
gamepad without disabling physical gamepad input.

### US-004-FR-005

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-05
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the keyboard button is pressed during gameplay, THE SYSTEM SHALL request
the real Android keyboard without blocking interaction outside the button.

### US-004-FR-006

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-06
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the IME closes or focus returns while the IME is closed, THE SYSTEM SHALL
restore fullscreen presentation while preserving the game's input path.

### US-004-FR-007

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-07
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user requests Play while client files are present, THE SYSTEM SHALL
prepare locally, reusing valid installed resources, without querying remote
versions or requiring a separate manual preparation action. File presence does
not prove integrity: strong admission and consent before launch are retained.
Ephemeral session state is obtained again; a previous reservation is not restored.

### US-004-FR-008

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-08
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE gameplay is active and the Android IME is visible, THE SYSTEM SHALL display
an app-owned accessory row above the IME, within the usable display area, with
Escape, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, the four direction keys, and a hide control visibly
labeled “Ocultar” with the accessible label “Ocultar teclado”; each control SHALL
have a touch target of at least 48 dp.

### US-004-FR-009

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-08
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user taps Escape, Tab, or a direction key in the accessory row, THE
SYSTEM SHALL send the corresponding key press and release to the game without
dismissing the IME.

### US-004-FR-010

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-08
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user taps Ctrl or Alt in the accessory row, THE SYSTEM SHALL toggle that
modifier's pressed state in the game and retain it for subsequent key input until
the user toggles it again.

### US-004-FR-011

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-08
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the IME closes or the game exits, THE SYSTEM SHALL release any Ctrl or Alt
modifier that remains pressed.

### US-004-FR-012

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-08
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user taps the accessory row's “Ocultar” control (accessible label
“Ocultar teclado”), THE SYSTEM SHALL close the Android IME without relying on the
IME's own hide affordance.

### US-004-FR-013

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-09
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user requests Play while an unfinished guest reservation remains,
THE SYSTEM SHALL attempt recovery only for the selected, matching storage,
using the existing exclusion and ownership checks and a fresh proof that its
runtime is quiescent. Only completed recovery permits fresh preparation,
validation, consent, and guest admission; no old ticket is reused as launch
authorization.

### US-004-FR-014

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-10
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE a game shutdown is processed, THE SYSTEM SHALL attempt to close its
owned reservation only after proven quiescence. Active or unknown runtime
state SHALL retain exclusion and block a new launch, with distinct brief
messages and a retry path. Recovery SHALL run off the UI thread and SHALL NOT
be duplicated by concurrent Play requests; it SHALL NOT recover an updater
reservation, force processes, reset storage, or bypass a changed destination
or ticket ownership.

Confirmed owned Z processes with stable PID/starttime/UID and Threads:1 in both
fresh group-status reads
SHALL be treated as terminated without requiring corpse exe/cmdline. State or
identity changes SHALL retain exclusion. THE SYSTEM SHALL request normal audio
shutdown through its own session control channel off the UI thread with a bounded
wait; it SHALL NOT exempt live audio by name or infer exit from request success.
Existing boot checks, two observations, ownership and updater exclusion remain.

### US-004-FR-015

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-03
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN a subsequent session is prepared or its resolution is applied, THE SYSTEM
SHALL retain graphics settings saved by the game in the launcher-owned profile,
including quality, shadows, and FPS limits, instead of rebuilding those settings
from the read-only source configuration. App reopening and APK updates SHALL NOT
implicitly reset an existing profile. The selected resolution remains launcher-owned.

### US-004-FR-016

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-11
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user chooses “Restablecer gráficos”, IF the game is closed and existing
storage/exclusion guards permit the operation, THE SYSTEM SHALL restore only the
initial graphics preset for the next launch, preserving resolution, account,
controls, server, storage, unrelated configuration, and client data. THE SYSTEM
SHALL NOT write settings belonging to an active game session. Before writing, a
matching unfinished guest reservation SHALL be recovered using the existing
destination, ownership, and fresh quiescence checks without requiring Play first.
Updater, mismatched, active, or unproven runtime state SHALL retain exclusion and
prevent the reset; no processes SHALL be forced or lock files deleted.

### US-004-FR-017

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-07, US-004-AC-11
- **Pattern:** Complex

WHEN the user enters resolution settings or confirms a graphics reset, IF an idle
unlaunched preparation remains, THE SYSTEM SHALL discard its snapshot and consent
automatically and continue the requested action without an internal-preparation
blocking message. Actual preparation, pending launch IO, and conflicting operations
SHALL remain excluded until finished; an old result SHALL NOT authorize a late
launch or reset. A subsequent Play SHALL obtain fresh preparation and consent.

### US-004-FR-018

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-12
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE a launch attempt is current, THE SYSTEM SHALL expose its brief memory-patch
state without presenting a scan finding, diagnostic capture, or expired monitoring
as a currently verified application. Terminal reporting SHALL distinguish no verified
application from previously verified application and SHALL preserve error reporting
when closure fails. Results belonging to a superseded attempt SHALL NOT replace the
current state. Status reporting SHALL NOT authorize writes or disclose secrets,
memory contents, or addresses.

WHILE an attempt is waiting for its search gate, THE SYSTEM SHALL report pending
search rather than searching; searching SHALL begin only when reading starts.

Late evidence for the same attempt SHALL refine its terminal history or error
without reactivating monitoring or presenting an ended session as live verification.
Exactly-once native closure does not forbid correcting an advisory terminal summary.

### US-004-FR-019

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-12
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN a game launch fails, THE SYSTEM SHALL cancel its owned patch session even if
normal guest-exit callbacks never occur. Repeated terminal callbacks SHALL NOT end
the same patch session more than once or create a new one. Failure to restore native
state SHALL retain existing write-closed exclusion rather than force another launch.

### US-004-FR-020

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-12
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the current attempt's WoW main window is identified, mapped with geometry
compatible with its frozen display plan, and has content activity, THE SYSTEM SHALL
record observed WoW startup and arm non-capture patch searching once without
requiring Enter. Property, mapping and content observations SHALL be correlated
even when delivered in a different order. Wine, desktop, transient windows and
observations from an old attempt SHALL NOT arm the search.

Observed startup SHALL NOT substitute for native ownership, key-store validation,
application verification, pixels presented, login readiness or authentication.
The native lease still begins before Wine. The existing 900000ms total bound and
180000ms active bound SHALL be preserved, with the latter measured from this
single arm and clamped to the total; later events SHALL NOT extend either bound.
Without the signal the search SHALL remain pending until canceled or expired.
Capture, native code and the polling interval SHALL remain unchanged. Bounded
relative monotonic timings SHALL distinguish the signal, search and apply without
logging window titles, key input, credentials, addresses or memory contents.

### US-004-FR-021

- **Derives from:** US-004-AC-12
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE a patch attempt is current, THE SYSTEM SHALL retain a bounded typed summary
of its scan scope, relative monotonic search/first-candidate/first-application times,
poll/pass counts, actual memory bytes read, read errors and nativeApply outcomes.
The existing assisted credential submission, if performed for that attempt, SHALL
be recorded as a submission milestone without recording account values or asserting
authentication. Manual Enter SHALL NOT be inferred as Login.

Superseded attempts SHALL NOT overwrite the latest summary. The summary SHALL NOT
contain raw native JSON, memory, keys, addresses, credentials, arguments, client logs
or screenshots. Its collection SHALL NOT add user gates or input automation, affect
write authorization, or make a failed summary write block patching or submission.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **US-004-NFR-001**: this UX change does not alter the runtime/driver, executable,
  Forever contract, TLS validation, or memory instrumentation.
- **US-004-NFR-002**: proprietary components and APK artifacts remain outside Git;
  builds are audited before installation. Never overwrite unrelated sources.
- **US-004-NFR-003**: do not present bootstrap, a live process, or transient Login
  as evidence of a complete installation, authentication, or stable gameplay.
- **US-004-NFR-004**: preparation avoids redundant full-tree walks at intermediate
  stages; it retains NOFOLLOW traversal when resolving and before launch, with
  revalidation of critical identities/paths during intermediate operations.
  Optimization does not replace hashes with size/mtime checks. Stage durations
  are logged without promising unmeasured device performance or expanding visible
  launcher text.

  Given a current patch attempt with native scan and apply results
  When results and an optional existing assisted submission arrive
  Then its summary records eligible scan scope, counters and relative timings
  And a scan alone never becomes a verified nativeApply outcome
  And submission is a milestone rather than successful authentication
  And raw errors, account values, keys, addresses and raw logs are absent
  And late results from an old attempt cannot overwrite the new summary
  And summary storage failure does not block patching or credential submission

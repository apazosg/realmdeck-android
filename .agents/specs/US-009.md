# US-009: Reuse an Existing ARM64 Client

Source plan: [existing-install-import-plan.md](../planning/existing-install-import-plan.md)
(state: `approved`). User decision (2026-10-20): the flow is “prepare folder” +
review, not “choose a path”; paste **only the ARM64 client**, and the app **reviews**
the pasted content and applies an update **if appropriate**.

This complements US-007 (storage destination) and US-008 (download/update): pasting
is an explicit user action at the destination already governed by US-007, and reuse
relies on the object-based download/update
defined in US-008. It does not relax US-007-FR-001 (SAF is not an executable path)
or US-007-FR-004 (no automatic copying, migration, or download).

**Approved portability behavior (2026-10-27):** the user explicitly requested
moving the USB between phones and chose both completed-client reuse and continuing
a partial download, then selected **“Aprobar criterios”** for UC-05 and AC-06 through
AC-09. Existing AC-01 through AC-05 remain unchanged. The user subsequently selected
**“Aprobar”** for FR-009 through FR-013, NFR-004 and the recovery design on 2026-10-27.
TC-007 through TC-013 were approved on 2026-10-28 for local implementation and tests;
they are not implemented or executed coverage. The user subsequently authorized building,
installing and testing audited APKs on the connected ADB WiFi device. Actual adoption
and payload retain the fresh plan and consent gates. The recovery route requires reconciliation
with US-008; ordinary foreign-stage checks stay fail-closed.
See the
[cross-phone plan](../planning/usb-cross-phone-plan.md) and its observed storage
selection correction, and the
[recovery design proposal](../../docs/usb-portability-recovery-design.md).

## As a

user of the Android launcher

## I want

to paste an ARM64 client installation that I already have onto USB and have the
app review and reuse it, and to move that USB to another compatible phone with a
complete client or a partial download

## So that

I do not have to download tens of gigabytes again when that content is already on
my drive.

## Use Cases

- **US-009-UC-01**: Prepare the folder where the client must be pasted and show
  exactly what to paste and where.
- **US-009-UC-02**: Review the pasted content and determine whether it matches,
  before changing anything.
- **US-009-UC-03**: Apply an update by reusing pasted content and downloading only
  what is missing.
- **US-009-UC-04**: Reject an incompatible source without modifying the client.
- **US-009-UC-05**: Move the same USB to another compatible Android
  phone, review an existing complete client or partial updater staging there,
  and reuse verified data without depending on the original phone's private state.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **US-009-AC-01** (UC-01): the app creates the client root on the selected
  removable volume and writes an instruction file **outside that root**. It
  contains the literal paste path, the equivalent path as seen from a PC, the
  expected build, and what not to paste. The paste location is the client root the
  app already uses on that volume; no broad storage permission is requested and no
  content is copied.
- **US-009-AC-02** (UC-02): review is read-only and returns a verdict with detected
  build, platform, and product. It distinguishes: nothing pasted; content that is
  not a readable CASC source; different product or region; x86_64 client; ARM64
  client at the target build; ARM64 client at the known prior build; and ARM64
  client at an unknown build (admitted only as a CASC source). A pasted client is
  never called “installed” without verification.
- **US-009-AC-03** (UC-03): after confirmation, only missing or corrupt objects are
  downloaded; present objects are reused after EKey, BLTE, CKey, and size checks.
  The cost shown before consent is the actual delta, not the full client size.
  Detecting the target build from metadata does not guarantee a zero delta: objects,
  loose files, or metadata may be missing.
- **US-009-AC-04** (UC-04): incompatible content is rejected **without writing
  anything** to the client and with a message explaining what to correct. A root
  with unrelated entries is reported with their list; they are not deleted. An
  unrelated loose file that collides with the destination and has no ownership
  proof blocks the operation. Source `.build.info` may be replaced when publishing
  the destination.
- **US-009-AC-05** (UC-03): preserve `WTF`, originals, accounts, and unrelated files;
  do not infer ownership of loose files merely because they were pasted. Retain
  storage-marker, symlink, hash, fsync, checkpoint, and journal invariants.
- **US-009-AC-06** (UC-05): after a safe USB transfer, the second phone
  can select the compatible Android-permitted app root through US-007 and review
  an existing complete ARM64 client under AC-02. Moving phones alone does not
  require copying the client, redownloading verified present data, or accessing
  the original phone's private files. Runtime preparation and launch retain their
  own compatibility and admission checks; storage recognition is not proof of Play.
- **US-009-AC-07** (UC-05): review distinguishes partial updater staging
  from a published client and an empty destination. It does not label partial
  content installed, complete, or ready to play. Opening the app, selecting the
  destination, or the read-only review does not adopt or overwrite that staging or
  start network work. Unverified progress is shown as unknown, not as an invented
  reusable-object count.
- **US-009-AC-08** (UC-05): an explicit continuation flow may establish
  new owned resume state on the second phone from objects verified against its
  officially admitted target catalog by EKey, BLTE, CKey, and sizes. It must not
  depend on copying the original private checkpoint/receipt or trusting a stage
  marker as an object-integrity proof. Metadata network work requires its existing
  disclosure/consent; client mutation and payload transfer require a confirmed
  plan showing actual missing/corrupt data and peak space. Already verified reusable
  data is preserved rather than automatically downloading the whole client again.
  If safe reuse cannot be established, preserve the source and explain the blocker
  without a destructive fallback.
- **US-009-AC-09** (UC-05): cancellation, disconnection, or failure during
  partial adoption preserves recoverable verified data and never activates an
  incomplete build. After a subsequent move, stale private state on either phone
  cannot authorize overwriting newer USB progress. Revalidation or explicit recovery
  is required; normal foreign-stage checks remain fail-closed outside the new
  admitted flow. Storage identity, no-follow, resource bounds, durability,
  Wi-Fi-only, consent, and guest/updater exclusion remain in force, without root
  or a broad storage permission.

## Functional Requirements

### US-009-FR-001

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-01
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user requests folder preparation, THE SYSTEM SHALL create the client
root on the selected removable volume and write the instructions in the parent
directory, **outside the client root**. If the root already contains files, it
SHALL preserve them; if it does not exist, it SHALL create an empty root for
pasting.

### US-009-FR-002

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-01
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user requests folder preparation, THE SYSTEM SHALL display the literal
paste path on the phone and its equivalent USB path as seen from the PC, as well
as the expected client version.

### US-009-FR-003

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-02
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user requests review of the pasted client, THE SYSTEM SHALL read
`.build.info` and the destination's CASC source structure without writing to the
client, accessing the network, or scanning the complete catalog.

### US-009-FR-004

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-02
- **Pattern:** Conditional

WHEN the pasted client is not an admitted ARM64 client for the supported product,
region, and language, or its CASC source is unreadable, THE SYSTEM SHALL return
the “does not match” verdict with the specific cause.

### US-009-FR-005

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-02, US-009-AC-03
- **Pattern:** Conditional

IF the pasted client is ARM64 for a build that is neither the target nor a known
prior build, THE SYSTEM SHALL admit it only as a source of CASC objects, verify
each object by EKey, BLTE, CKey, and size against the target catalog, and download
the target build's loose files itself. This does not authorize overwriting
unrelated loose files that collide with the target.

### US-009-FR-006

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-03
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user confirms a plan for an installation using a pasted client, THE
SYSTEM SHALL download only missing or corrupt objects, reuse present verified
objects, and show the actual delta cost before requesting consent.

### US-009-FR-007

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-04
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN review detects unrelated entries in the client root, THE SYSTEM SHALL report
them without deleting, overwriting, or claiming ownership of them.

### US-009-FR-008

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-05
- **Pattern:** Ubiquitous

THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve `WTF`, originals, and unrelated files; claim ownership
only of content it writes or verifies as belonging to the target build; and retain
storage-marker, symlink, hash, fsync, checkpoint, and journal invariants.

### Portability requirements — approved, implementation pending

The following requirements derive from the approved portability criteria. Their
approval does not authorize adoption on existing USB data: test-case approval and
verified implementation are still pending.

### US-009-FR-009

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-06
- **Pattern:** Event-driven
- **Status:** [APPROVED — 2026-10-27; implementation pending]

WHEN the user requests review of a complete client at a compatible USB root on
another phone, THE SYSTEM SHALL use FR-003 through FR-008 without requiring the
original phone's private files, duplicating the client, or redownloading verified
present data solely because the phone changed. Runtime admission SHALL remain a
separate prerequisite for Play.

### US-009-FR-010

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-07
- **Pattern:** Event-driven
- **Status:** [APPROVED — 2026-10-27; implementation pending]

WHEN the app inspects or locally reviews the selected destination, THE SYSTEM SHALL
distinguish an empty destination, a published client, partial updater staging, and
unreadable or ambiguous pending data using bounded local structure checks. It SHALL
NOT adopt that staging, change its ownership, scan all object bodies, or start
network work as a side effect of app
opening, destination selection, or this review. Unverified reusable counts SHALL
remain unknown and partial staging SHALL NOT be presented as installed or playable.

### US-009-FR-011

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-07, US-009-AC-08
- **Pattern:** Event-driven
- **Status:** [APPROVED — 2026-10-27; implementation pending]

WHEN the user explicitly requests verification for partial continuation, THE SYSTEM
SHALL use its officially admitted target catalog and authenticate every candidate
object counted as reusable by its full EKey, BLTE structure/chunk checksums, CKey,
and encoded/decoded sizes. Marker presence, file size, or a claimed original object
count SHALL NOT substitute for those checks. Any required metadata network work
SHALL follow existing disclosure, consent, and Wi-Fi rules. This verification SHALL
preserve existing client/staging bytes and SHALL NOT transfer game payload.

### US-009-FR-012

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-08
- **Pattern:** Conditional
- **Status:** [APPROVED — 2026-10-27; implementation pending]

WHEN the user confirms a partial-continuation plan, IF destination, admitted
catalog, verified source state, consent, and available space are still valid, THE
SYSTEM SHALL establish new owned resume state from the verified data and continue
only the required missing/corrupt data. The plan SHALL disclose reusable data,
payload delta, and peak space including recovery metadata and temporaries before
client mutation or payload transfer. Copying the original private checkpoint or
receipt SHALL NOT be required or used as the integrity proof. If safe reuse cannot
be established, THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve the source and explain the blocker,
without an automatic destructive full-download fallback.

### US-009-FR-013

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-08, US-009-AC-09
- **Pattern:** State-driven
- **Status:** [APPROVED — 2026-10-27; implementation pending]

WHILE partial adoption or its recovery is unfinished, THE SYSTEM SHALL retain
guest/updater exclusion and recover through identity-bound durable state before
normal append or activation. Cancellation, failure, or USB removal SHALL preserve
recoverable verified data. A stale private receipt on either phone SHALL NOT
authorize resetting, truncating, or overwriting newer USB progress; revalidation or
explicit recovery is required. Ordinary foreign-stage rejection SHALL remain in
force outside the admitted flow, and publication SHALL still require the complete
verified target and existing transaction ordering.

## Non-Functional Requirements

### US-009-NFR-001

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-02

Review of a pasted client does not download network bytes or scan the full catalog;
its cost is limited to reading `.build.info` and the destination's CASC source
structure.

### US-009-NFR-002

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-01

The feature requires no broad storage permission: it operates on the client root
on removable storage already used by the app, does not present SAF as an executable
path, and does not copy the client automatically.

### US-009-NFR-003

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-03

The resulting operation respects existing network-consent, storage, and memory
limits for client downloads.

### US-009-NFR-004

- **Derives from:** US-009-AC-07, US-009-AC-08, US-009-AC-09
- **Status:** [APPROVED — 2026-10-27; implementation pending]

Partial discovery SHALL remain bounded metadata work. Full verification/recovery
SHALL run off the UI thread with streams or bounded disk-backed state, without
retaining the full object catalog or decoded payload in RAM. Its disk work and
temporaries SHALL be accounted for in the plan; verified/reused bytes SHALL NOT be
reported as network speed. Existing US-008 resource, storage-validation, durability,
no-follow, and process-exclusion limits remain applicable.

## Assumptions

- **[ASSUMPTION]** The paste destination is the client root on the removable
  volume (`Android/data/<package>/files/CustomGames/WoW Forever/` on USB), because
  it is the app-specific path already used without broad storage permission and
  mapped to Wine `A:`. A PC that mounts the USB directly can create/paste there;
  Android 11+ does not expose this path over MTP.
- **[ASSUMPTION]** Pasted content is limited to `Data/` and `.build.info`
  (required). The ARM64 executable and dependencies should be pasted only if they
  exactly match the target; prior versions may block on collision. Do not copy
  hidden internal app state. Report additional files; do not delete them.
- **[ASSUMPTION]** An unknown-build source is reusable because CASC objects are
  content-addressed by EKey and each is revalidated against the target catalog.

## Open Questions

- TC-007 through TC-013 were approved on 2026-10-28 after requirements and design
  approval. Candidate APK creation, installation and testing on the connected device
  are also authorized; preserve the audited artifact and actual adoption-plan gates.
  Existing TC-001 through TC-006 do not claim partial-adoption coverage.
- **[PENDING]** Implement and verify the approved recovery design and test cases:
  bounded enumeration, ownership generation, private-intent recovery,
  admission of existing segments, and stale-state rejection before and after
  publication. Current APIs require a private identity-bound checkpoint; a new flow
  must not call ordinary tail truncation on unadmitted foreign files. Unsupported
  layouts remain preserved with an explicit blocker; no implementation is claimed.
- **[PENDING]** Reconcile the admitted adoption entry point with US-008-FR-004 and
  its existing staging/journal guards; no change to normal Resume is implied by
  approval of the portability criteria alone.
- The paste path is not visible over MTP on Android 11+; physical device testing
  must confirm the folder survives mounting and is not removed by the file
  manager. If not, the alternative instructions may need to use `adb push`.
- Observed behavior indicates unrelated entries from an unknown build can block
  admission, and unrelated loose files colliding with the target block updates.
  Publication does not delete other entries. Do not promise admission of a whole
  client from any version.
- `[PENDING]` The physical test for importing a separate pasted installation has
  not been completed. C117 publication of build 70450 and the C127 client trial
  are not evidence that importing a separate copy works. Do not infer a source
  workflow or add a host command without separate approval.

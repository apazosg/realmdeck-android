# US-008: Download and Update the ARM64 Client

> **Pending specification reconciliation (2026-10-23):** the user confirmed
> continuing the agreed Home installation/resumption flow and Wi-Fi-only scope.
> FR-001/002/004 and FR-008 through FR-012 formalize that behavior.
> Those requirements, AC-12 and TC-013 through TC-018 were approved on
> 2026-10-23. Implementation and device validation are in progress. AC-12's
> optimizations retain FR-003's bounded verified reuse; before/after measurement
> belongs to the engineering validation plan, not a new end-user benchmark feature.
> Specification approval is not a claim of completed device acceptance or speedup.

**Approved behavior:** new versions must be prepared and updated from the same
APK, without recompiling it for each build. Packaged admission data is a fallback,
not the normal flow; consent, recovery, integrity, and mutual exclusion remain
required. See the
[approved plan](../planning/launcher-on-device-updates-plan.md).

## As a

user of the Android launcher

## I want

to check versions and download, verify, repair, or update the client at the chosen
location, with accurate costs and progress

## So that

I do not need to copy files manually or routinely duplicate the installation to
update it.

This derives from the [approved plan](../planning/launcher-client-updater-plan.md)
and the user's requests to build the downloader/updater. The user confirmed that
build 70425 is used by Classic Forever; that confirmation is not this project's
own login test and does not approve every published build/architecture. The first
admission target was 70425 Windows/ARM64/enUS, with known source build 70009. The
APK obtains/verifies official metadata and generates a local catalog for another
supported build without a PC or another app build. A packaged catalog may be a
fallback, not a per-version requirement. The APK does not include game PE/CASC
payloads. Changes to unsupported formats/capabilities may require a launcher update.

> **Historical operational note, not a new requirement:** C112 was an earlier
> checkpoint for 70450 on USB, not proof of a completed installation. C117 later
> completed publication of 70450 ARM64/enUS, and C127 physically reached the
> Classic Forever login and in-game entry on the xaga/Mali device. C128/C129 were
> source-reviewed and audited but not installed. These observations do not close
> end-to-end updater/recovery acceptance. See the
> [current launcher status](../../docs/launcher-app-status.md) and
> [downloader handoff](../../docs/downloader-handoff.md) for dated evidence.

## Use Cases

- **US-008-UC-01**: Inspect client state/version and manually check for updates.
- **US-008-UC-02**: Calculate download/space, obtain consent, then download/resume.
- **US-008-UC-03**: Verify/repair objects and recover an interrupted activation.
- **US-008-UC-04**: Install or resume within Home with few decisions,
  meaningful preparation feedback, and download speed/time estimates.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **US-008-AC-01** (UC-01): distinct states for matching metadata, a candidate
  without ARM64 evidence, an admitted destination, and an error/unknown installed
  identity. Do not claim “Up to date” after a network failure or from a bootstrap
  alone. The same APK resolves/admit two supported builds from official metadata
  without regenerating assets or depending on an unverified host/third-party catalog.
- **US-008-AC-02** (UC-02): destination, version, network cost, and peak space per
  volume are visible; explicit approval is bound to the plan before downloading or
  writing the client.
- **US-008-AC-03** (UC-02): reuse only destination objects verified by complete EKey,
  sizes, and CKey; download missing/corrupt objects. Memory/queues are bounded and
  new segments are proportional to the delta, without copying/hard-linking old CASC.
- **US-008-AC-04** (UC-02, UC-03): pause/restart preserves verifiable progress;
  the journal recovers a coherent build. Do not publish a mixed build or activate
  partial staging.
  The approved directed sourceless storage cadence in US-007-AC-05 permits
  intermediate staging writes and deferred full storage checks. A detected
  boundary-check failure prevents that group's checkpoint acknowledgement and
  retirement of its raw sources; this does not promise detection of every
   concurrent pathname substitution before a write.
  Durable commit groups may retain up to sixteen objects, with smaller flushes
  under pressure, rollover or completion. This increases unacknowledged staging
  and the interval between lightweight group checks; acknowledged progress and
  the 34-key/1-GiB raw limits remain protected.
- **US-008-AC-05** (UC-03): live Wine, unknown closure, pending journal, or changed
  storage identity blocks the corresponding mutation/start, including after Activity
  recreation or host death. A matching old guest reservation may be recovered on
  explicit Play only after fresh quiescence and existing ownership/exclusion
  checks; launch remains blocked until recovery completes and fresh admission
  succeeds. This route does not recover updater reservations or journals.
  No root or new storage permissions.
  The shared shutdown proof treats only stable owned Z processes as terminated
  under US-004-AC-10; this does not exempt live audio or weaken updater exclusion.
- **US-008-AC-06** (UC-01, UC-03): installation/completeness and startup/login tests
  are distinct; pending native acceptance is reported as a warning and does not veto
  execution. Physical operations require usable storage and consent. Preserve
  originals/configuration/accounts and unrelated files.
- **US-008-AC-07** (UC-02, UC-04): new installation is integrated into Home rather
  than a modal manager or a separate installation screen. The primary area presents
  the current installation step and its corresponding action. With a valid
  destination already selected and no exceptional
  error/permission decision, starting the game download takes at most three user
  actions from Home: open installation, Continue, and confirm Download. Continue
  explicitly authorizes version lookup and metadata preparation, with its network
  cost disclosed before that action; those steps run consecutively without more
  routine confirmations. Opening the app or installation screen alone does not
  query remote versions or start metadata/payload downloads. The final confirmation
  shows destination, version, game-download cost and additional space and remains
  bound to the current plan under AC-02. Existing update/repair behavior is not
  redesigned by this criterion.
- **US-008-AC-08** (UC-02, UC-04): preparation shows a human-readable current phase
  (version lookup, metadata transfer/validation, catalog construction, or planning)
  and the real counters available for that phase, including a processed count when
  no total is known. Counters retain their correct units; unknown totals do not
  produce invented percentages. Preparation can be cancelled outside protected
  atomic phases without discarding durable verifiable progress. Long explanations,
  full paths and diagnostics are available in Details; required costs, actionable
  warnings and errors remain visible without opening Details.
- **US-008-AC-09** (UC-02, UC-04): during game payload transfer the screen shows
  transferred/total bytes, a smoothed transfer speed and an estimated remaining
  download time based on actual network transfer, not reused or locally verified
  content. Until sufficient samples exist, or while transfer is stalled/paused,
  estimates are explicitly unavailable rather than stale or fabricated. Verification
  and activation are labeled separately and are not represented as measured network
  transfer or as included in the remaining download-time estimate.
- **US-008-AC-10** (UC-02, UC-04): Pause preserves verifiable progress; Resume is
  one explicit action for an unchanged, still-valid consented plan. If consent,
  cost, destination or plan revision is no longer valid, calculation and explicit
  confirmation are required again under AC-02. Navigating back during a foreground
  transfer does not implicitly pause it; returning to Home reconnects
  to the running operation without starting another. Completion offers Play, with
  all existing admission and launch guards intact.
- **US-008-AC-11** (UC-01, UC-02, UC-03, UC-04): client downloads, including version
  metadata, catalog preparation and repair/update payloads, use Wi-Fi only. Without
  usable Wi-Fi, network work is blocked with an actionable waiting/paused state;
  losing Wi-Fi interrupts transfers without cellular fallback and preserves durable
  verifiable progress. Network eligibility does not replace plan consent or any
  resume/integrity/storage guard. Local inspection and already-prepared Play are
  not blocked merely because Wi-Fi is absent. This policy covers app-managed
  transfers, not network traffic generated by the running game or an external browser.
- **US-008-AC-12** (UC-02, UC-04): improve actual client-file
  download speed as far as measured bottlenecks permit, not merely perceived
  progress. Evaluate useful network throughput and preparation, transfer,
  verification and write durations separately under comparable before/after
  conditions, distinguishing local reuse and retries from useful network bytes.
  Optimizations retain bounded resource use, integrity checks, durable resumability,
  Wi-Fi-only, consent and storage/Wine guards. No fixed throughput or speedup is
  promised without evidence; synthetic/emulator measurements are not proof of
  full-client performance on a physical USB destination.

## Functional Requirements

### US-008-FR-001

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-01, US-008-AC-07
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user checks versions, THE SYSTEM SHALL compare official metadata
fingerprints and architecture evidence without modifying the client or starting
payload downloads.

The remote query requires “Check for updates” or the new-installation “Continue”
action after disclosure of metadata network cost; it does not run merely when
opening the app or installation UI. Finding a new version or failing the query
does not require an update and does not block Play with an admitted installation;
local guards remain in force.

WHEN a new supported destination is prepared, THE SYSTEM SHALL verify the official
config/manifest/index chain on Android and generate its local catalog with bounded
work and persistent metadata caching, without running host tools.

### US-008-FR-002

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-02, US-008-AC-07, US-008-AC-10
- **Pattern:** Conditional

WHEN the user confirms a plan, IF destination identity, revision, consent, and
available space are still valid, THE SYSTEM SHALL start the operation at the
selected destination; otherwise it SHALL require a fresh calculation without
silent fallback.

### US-008-FR-003

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-03, US-008-AC-12
- **Pattern:** Ubiquitous

THE SYSTEM SHALL build canonical indexes for the complete destination manifest
using verified content, not an indiscriminate union with the old cache.

Ordered local entries or guarded pages may be used only as location hints; the
system SHALL NOT infer semantics from unknown cache states. A hint does not prove
availability: verify the physical header, full EKey, BLTE, and CKey. Unrepresentable
destination prefix conflicts or unsupported structural formats block the operation
without ignoring sections or deleting previous data.

### US-008-FR-004

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-04, US-008-AC-10
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN an operation is interrupted, THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve its own durable
checkpoints and recover/resume only after checking identity/content; `.build.info`
is published only after other admitted transaction files.

During directed sourceless installation, THE SYSTEM SHALL apply the approved
US-007-FR-005 full/lightweight boundaries before group durability. A failed check
SHALL prevent the group's checkpoint append, acknowledgement and raw retirement;
already-written unacknowledged staging may remain for guarded recovery. Per-object
authentication, force/checkpoint order and checkpoint identity/format remain
unchanged. The subsequently approved maximum commit group is sixteen objects,
with partial flushes under pressure, rollover and completion. Raw reservations
remain bounded at 34 keys and 1 GiB; sources SHALL remain retained until data
and checkpoint forces succeed. This amendment was approved on 2026-10-25; implementation
and device acceptance are not implied by specification approval.

### US-008-FR-005

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-05
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE a Wine session is active/unknown or recovery is pending, THE SYSTEM SHALL
retain durable exclusion and reject incompatible actions.
Stable owned zombie recognition follows US-004-FR-014 without requiring corpse
exe/cmdline; unreadable live processes, state/identity races, or remaining live
audio SHALL NOT prove quiescence or release an updater reservation.

### US-008-FR-006

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-06
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE native acceptance is missing, THE SYSTEM SHALL display it as pending without
using it to veto download/activation; consent, integrity, and Wine exclusion remain
required. An unusable filesystem does block the corresponding operation.

### US-008-FR-007

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-05
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user requests explicit Play while a matching old guest reservation
remains, THE SYSTEM SHALL recover it only after fresh quiescence and the
existing ownership and exclusion checks; launch remains blocked until recovery
completes and fresh admission succeeds. This route SHALL NOT recover updater
reservations or journals.

### US-008-FR-008

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-07, US-008-AC-08
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user opens new installation from Home, THE SYSTEM SHALL present the
installation flow within Home, without a modal manager or separate screen.
With a valid selected destination and no exceptional decision, at most three
actions SHALL start payload transfer: open installation, Continue, and Download.
Continue SHALL disclose metadata network cost before starting consecutive version
lookup and preparation; Download SHALL require the current destination, version,
payload network cost and additional-space summary under FR-002. Details SHALL
contain extended explanations, paths and diagnostics without hiding required costs,
actionable warnings or errors. Update/repair UX remains outside this redesign.

### US-008-FR-009

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-08
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE installation preparation runs, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the current phase and
available real counters with phase-appropriate units, including completed counts
when totals are unknown, without invented percentages. Cancellation SHALL remain
available outside protected atomic phases and preserve durable verifiable progress.

### US-008-FR-010

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-09
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE game payload transfer runs, THE SYSTEM SHALL display transferred/total bytes,
smoothed network speed in MB/s and estimated remaining download time from actual
network samples, excluding reused or locally verified content. Insufficient samples,
pause or stalled transfer SHALL make estimates explicitly unavailable rather than
retain stale values. Verification and activation SHALL be labeled separately and
SHALL NOT be represented as network transfer or included in that download ETA.

### US-008-FR-011

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-10
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user pauses or resumes installation, THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve verifiable
progress and offer one-action Resume only for an unchanged, still-valid consented
plan; otherwise FR-002 SHALL require recalculation and explicit confirmation.
Back navigation during foreground payload transfer SHALL NOT implicitly pause it;
returning to Home SHALL reconnect to the existing operation without duplicating
work. Completion SHALL offer Play subject to existing admission and launch guards.

### US-008-FR-012

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-11
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE any app-managed client network work is requested or active, THE SYSTEM SHALL
restrict its connections to usable Wi-Fi, including version metadata, preparation,
installation, update and repair. Without usable Wi-Fi it SHALL block or interrupt
network work, explain the waiting/paused state and preserve durable verifiable
progress without cellular fallback. Reconnection SHALL NOT bypass consent,
integrity, storage or resume checks. Local inspection and already-prepared Play
SHALL NOT require Wi-Fi; running-game and external-browser traffic are excluded.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **US-008-NFR-001**: use SQLite/cursors or streams for catalogs/selections; do not
  retain millions of objects or futures in RAM. Bound and estimate disk work and
  temporaries; do not claim RSS equals the heap limit.
- **US-008-NFR-002**: use official HTTPS, strict response/range/size handling, and
  correct EKey BLTE verification (header + table for chunked objects; full object
  for headerless), chunk checksums, and independent CKey/decoded-size checks.
- **US-008-NFR-003**: use owned journals/leases, no-follow paths, and UUID staging
  with markers bound to identity and prior files admitted by fingerprint. No
  non-atomic rename fallback or deletion of unknown files to force recovery.
- **US-008-NFR-004**: keep client/large artifacts out of Git; reconstruct/audit
  source/assets/signature/JNI. Treat CASC changes as an admitted postimage without
  relaxing historical pins. Independently review integrity before activation.

## Open Questions

- AC-12 and the performance-plan extension were approved on 2026-10-23.
  TC-013 through TC-018 were also approved; UX/network implementation is under
  validation. Performance work preserves
  FR-003 and existing NFR; measurements belong to the approved validation plan.
  A current comparable throughput baseline is not yet available.

- The user approved Wi-Fi-only for all app downloads on 2026-10-23, including
  metadata and runtime. AC-11 records the client scope; US-003 and US-004 record
  runtime provisioning and APK-notice metadata respectively. FR-012 is approved;
  TC-018 is approved. Physical acceptance of the new enforcement remains pending.

- The user approved FR-001/002/004 and FR-008 through FR-012 on 2026-10-23.
  The corresponding new test cases were also approved; validation is in progress.
  Existing NFR remain applicable; no unmeasured performance target is added. The
  UX impact is limited to installation/resumption and the remote-query
  entry point; storage selection (US-007), pasted import (US-009), runtime guards
  and update/repair consent are unchanged. See the
   [download performance plan](../planning/download-bottlenecks-plan.md).
- No measured preparation-time baseline is available for the reported APK/device.
  Redundant-work optimizations require evidence; no fixed duration or ETA for
  catalog preparation is promised.

- End-to-end acceptance on Android, including recovery/publication, remains
  pending. Partial physical storage/resumption evidence in the
  [downloader handoff](../../docs/downloader-handoff.md) does not close it; host
  tests are not a substitute.
- The user confirmed 70425 as Forever-supported, but this project has not thereby
  established its own login result. Build 70450 has a user-confirmed login and
  in-game entry on one device; that does not establish a physical delta between
  complete installations or every supported-build combination. Consult the
  current status and handoff before asserting build-specific acceptance.
- Runtime provisioning on a new phone, sustained gameplay, and autologin are
  outside this story. Selecting a destination alone does not provide those
  components.

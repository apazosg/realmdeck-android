# US-008: Download and Update the ARM64 Client

**Approved behavior:** new versions must be prepared and updated from the same
APK, without recompiling it for each build. Packaged admission data is a fallback,
not the normal flow; consent, recovery, integrity, and mutual exclusion remain
required. See the
[approved plan](../planning/launcher-on-device-updates-plan.md).

## As a

user of the Android launcher

## I want

to check versions and download, verify, repair, or update the client at the chosen
location, with accurate costs and progress

## So that

I do not need to copy files manually or routinely duplicate the installation to
update it.

This derives from the [approved plan](../planning/launcher-client-updater-plan.md)
and the user's requests to build the downloader/updater. The user confirmed that
build 70425 is used by Classic Forever; that confirmation is not this project's
own login test and does not approve every published build/architecture. The first
admission target was 70425 Windows/ARM64/enUS, with known source build 70009. The
APK obtains/verifies official metadata and generates a local catalog for another
supported build without a PC or another app build. A packaged catalog may be a
fallback, not a per-version requirement. The APK does not include game PE/CASC
payloads. Changes to unsupported formats/capabilities may require a launcher update.

> **Historical operational note, not a new requirement:** C112 was an earlier
> checkpoint for 70450 on USB, not proof of a completed installation. C117 later
> completed publication of 70450 ARM64/enUS, and C127 physically reached the
> Classic Forever login and in-game entry on the xaga/Mali device. C128/C129 were
> source-reviewed and audited but not installed. These observations do not close
> end-to-end updater/recovery acceptance. See the
> [current launcher status](../../docs/launcher-app-status.md) and
> [downloader handoff](../../docs/downloader-handoff.md) for dated evidence.

## Use Cases

- **US-008-UC-01**: Inspect client state/version and manually check for updates.
- **US-008-UC-02**: Calculate download/space, obtain consent, then download/resume.
- **US-008-UC-03**: Verify/repair objects and recover an interrupted activation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **US-008-AC-01** (UC-01): distinct states for matching metadata, a candidate
  without ARM64 evidence, an admitted destination, and an error/unknown installed
  identity. Do not claim “Up to date” after a network failure or from a bootstrap
  alone. The same APK resolves/admit two supported builds from official metadata
  without regenerating assets or depending on an unverified host/third-party catalog.
- **US-008-AC-02** (UC-02): destination, version, network cost, and peak space per
  volume are visible; explicit approval is bound to the plan before downloading or
  writing the client.
- **US-008-AC-03** (UC-02): reuse only destination objects verified by complete EKey,
  sizes, and CKey; download missing/corrupt objects. Memory/queues are bounded and
  new segments are proportional to the delta, without copying/hard-linking old CASC.
- **US-008-AC-04** (UC-02, UC-03): pause/restart preserves verifiable progress;
  the journal recovers a coherent build. Do not publish a mixed build or activate
  partial staging.
- **US-008-AC-05** (UC-03): live Wine, unknown closure, pending journal, or changed
  storage identity blocks the corresponding mutation/start, including after Activity
  recreation or host death. A matching old guest reservation may be recovered on
  explicit Play only after fresh quiescence and existing ownership/exclusion
  checks; launch remains blocked until recovery completes and fresh admission
  succeeds. This route does not recover updater reservations or journals.
  No root or new storage permissions.
  The shared shutdown proof treats only stable owned Z processes as terminated
  under US-004-AC-10; this does not exempt live audio or weaken updater exclusion.
- **US-008-AC-06** (UC-01, UC-03): installation/completeness and startup/login tests
  are distinct; pending native acceptance is reported as a warning and does not veto
  execution. Physical operations require usable storage and consent. Preserve
  originals/configuration/accounts and unrelated files.

## Functional Requirements

### US-008-FR-001

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-01
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user checks versions, THE SYSTEM SHALL compare official metadata
fingerprints and architecture evidence without modifying the client or starting
payload downloads.

The remote query requires “Check for updates”; it does not run when opening the
app or manager. Finding a new version or failing the query does not require an
update and does not block Play with an admitted installation; local guards remain
in force.

WHEN a new supported destination is prepared, THE SYSTEM SHALL verify the official
config/manifest/index chain on Android and generate its local catalog with bounded
work and persistent metadata caching, without running host tools.

### US-008-FR-002

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-02
- **Pattern:** Conditional

WHEN the user confirms a plan, IF destination identity, revision, consent, and
available space are still valid, THE SYSTEM SHALL start the operation at the
selected destination; otherwise it SHALL require a fresh calculation without
silent fallback.

### US-008-FR-003

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-03
- **Pattern:** Ubiquitous

THE SYSTEM SHALL build canonical indexes for the complete destination manifest
using verified content, not an indiscriminate union with the old cache.

Ordered local entries or guarded pages may be used only as location hints; the
system SHALL NOT infer semantics from unknown cache states. A hint does not prove
availability: verify the physical header, full EKey, BLTE, and CKey. Unrepresentable
destination prefix conflicts or unsupported structural formats block the operation
without ignoring sections or deleting previous data.

### US-008-FR-004

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-04
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN an operation is interrupted, THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve its own durable
checkpoints and recover/resume only after checking identity/content; `.build.info`
is published only after other admitted transaction files.

### US-008-FR-005

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-05
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE a Wine session is active/unknown or recovery is pending, THE SYSTEM SHALL
retain durable exclusion and reject incompatible actions.
Stable owned zombie recognition follows US-004-FR-014 without requiring corpse
exe/cmdline; unreadable live processes, state/identity races, or remaining live
audio SHALL NOT prove quiescence or release an updater reservation.

### US-008-FR-006

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-06
- **Pattern:** State-driven

WHILE native acceptance is missing, THE SYSTEM SHALL display it as pending without
using it to veto download/activation; consent, integrity, and Wine exclusion remain
required. An unusable filesystem does block the corresponding operation.

### US-008-FR-007

- **Derives from:** US-008-AC-05
- **Pattern:** Event-driven

WHEN the user requests explicit Play while a matching old guest reservation
remains, THE SYSTEM SHALL recover it only after fresh quiescence and the
existing ownership and exclusion checks; launch remains blocked until recovery
completes and fresh admission succeeds. This route SHALL NOT recover updater
reservations or journals.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **US-008-NFR-001**: use SQLite/cursors or streams for catalogs/selections; do not
  retain millions of objects or futures in RAM. Bound and estimate disk work and
  temporaries; do not claim RSS equals the heap limit.
- **US-008-NFR-002**: use official HTTPS, strict response/range/size handling, and
  correct EKey BLTE verification (header + table for chunked objects; full object
  for headerless), chunk checksums, and independent CKey/decoded-size checks.
- **US-008-NFR-003**: use owned journals/leases, no-follow paths, and UUID staging
  with markers bound to identity and prior files admitted by fingerprint. No
  non-atomic rename fallback or deletion of unknown files to force recovery.
- **US-008-NFR-004**: keep client/large artifacts out of Git; reconstruct/audit
  source/assets/signature/JNI. Treat CASC changes as an admitted postimage without
  relaxing historical pins. Independently review integrity before activation.

## Open Questions

- End-to-end acceptance on Android, including recovery/publication, remains
  pending. Partial physical storage/resumption evidence in the
  [downloader handoff](../../docs/downloader-handoff.md) does not close it; host
  tests are not a substitute.
- The user confirmed 70425 as Forever-supported, but this project has not thereby
  established its own login result. Build 70450 has a user-confirmed login and
  in-game entry on one device; that does not establish a physical delta between
  complete installations or every supported-build combination. Consult the
  current status and handoff before asserting build-specific acceptance.
- Runtime provisioning on a new phone, sustained gameplay, and autologin are
  outside this story. Selecting a destination alone does not provide those
  components.
